# Literature Collection Roadmap

## Overview
This roadmap outlines the phases for building a comprehensive literature collection supporting the floppi project's calculator and flight systems development.

## Phase 1: Core Aerospace Foundation

### Fundamental Aerodynamics
- Acquire foundational aerodynamics textbooks
  - Anderson's "Fundamentals of Aerodynamics"
  - Katz and Plotkin's "Low-Speed Aerodynamics"
  - McCormick's "Aerodynamics, Aeronautics, and Flight Mechanics"
- Collect computational aerodynamics references
- Gather airfoil data and theory resources

### Flight Dynamics and Control
- Obtain aircraft dynamics textbooks
  - Stevens and Lewis "Aircraft Control and Simulation"
  - Etkin and Reid "Dynamics of Flight: Stability and Control"
  - Nelson "Flight Stability and Automatic Control"
- Collect control systems theory references
- Acquire stability and handling qualities documentation

### General Aerospace Engineering
- Gather aircraft design textbooks
  - Raymer's "Aircraft Design: A Conceptual Approach"
  - Roskam's aircraft design series
- Collect aerospace performance analysis materials
- Obtain atmospheric modeling references

## Phase 2: Rotary-Wing Specialization

### Helicopter and Rotor Theory
- Acquire comprehensive helicopter textbooks
  - Leishman's "Principles of Helicopter Aerodynamics"
  - Seddon and Newman's "Basic Helicopter Aerodynamics"
  - Prouty's "Helicopter Performance, Stability, and Control"
- Collect rotor dynamics references
- Gather blade element momentum theory materials

### Multi-Rotor Systems
- Obtain multi-rotor specific research papers
- Collect quadcopter dynamics and control literature
- Gather coaxial rotor configuration studies
- Acquire variable pitch rotor research

### Advanced Rotor Topics
- Collect rotor wake modeling literature
- Gather rotor-rotor interaction studies
- Obtain autorotation analysis references
- Acquire rotor optimization research papers

## Phase 3: VTOL and Drone Systems

### VTOL Aircraft Design
- Acquire VTOL-specific textbooks and references
- Collect tiltrotor aircraft literature
- Gather transition flight mechanics research
- Obtain hybrid propulsion VTOL studies

### Small UAS/Drone Literature
- Collect small unmanned aircraft design references
- Gather autonomous flight research papers
- Obtain sense-and-avoid technology literature
- Acquire formation flight and swarm research

### Mission Planning and Performance
- Collect flight planning algorithm literature
- Gather endurance and range optimization studies
- Obtain payload integration references
- Acquire environmental effects research

## Phase 4: Battery and Power Systems

### Battery Technology
- Acquire lithium-ion battery textbooks
  - Electrochemistry fundamentals
  - Battery materials science
- Collect battery modeling literature
- Gather degradation and aging studies

### Battery Management Systems
- Obtain BMS design references
- Collect state-of-charge estimation literature
- Gather thermal management research
- Acquire safety and protection system documentation

### Power Systems Integration
- Collect electric propulsion system literature
- Gather power distribution design references
- Obtain efficiency optimization studies
- Acquire power electronics for aviation references

## Phase 5: Mechanical Engineering

### Structural Analysis
- Acquire structural mechanics textbooks
  - Beam theory
  - Plate and shell theory
  - Finite element analysis
- Collect vibration analysis references
- Gather fatigue and fracture mechanics literature

### Materials Science
- Obtain materials selection references
- Collect composite materials literature
  - Carbon fiber design and analysis
  - Sandwich structures
- Gather material properties databases
- Acquire manufacturing process documentation

### Mechanical Design
- Collect machine design references
- Gather mechanism and kinematics literature
- Obtain CAD and design optimization resources
- Acquire tolerance analysis documentation

## Phase 6: Electrical Components Engineering

### Motor Technology
- Acquire brushless motor theory textbooks
- Collect motor design and analysis literature
- Gather motor control algorithm references
- Obtain motor efficiency optimization studies

### Power Electronics
- Collect power converter design literature
- Gather ESC (Electronic Speed Controller) references
- Obtain switching regulator design resources
- Acquire electromagnetic compatibility (EMC) documentation

### Sensors and Instrumentation
- Collect IMU theory and calibration literature
- Gather GPS and positioning system references
- Obtain pressure sensor and airspeed references
- Acquire sensor fusion algorithm literature

### Control Systems Implementation
- Collect embedded control system references
- Gather real-time systems literature
- Obtain microcontroller-specific resources
- Acquire communication protocols documentation

## Phase 7: Organization and Knowledge Management

### Literature Cataloging
- Develop consistent naming and filing system
- Create bibliographic database
- Tag resources by topic and application
- Cross-reference related materials

### Annotation and Summarization
- Annotate key chapters and sections
- Create summary notes for critical concepts
- Highlight relevant equations and derivations
- Note practical applications and limitations

### Reference Guide Development
- Extract key equations by topic area
- Create quick reference tables
  - Standard atmosphere properties
  - Common aerodynamic coefficients
  - Battery specifications
  - Material properties
- Develop calculation flowcharts
- Document unit conversions and standards

### Integration with Project Tools
- Link literature references to calculator equations
- Document theoretical basis for flight system algorithms
- Create design guideline documents
- Develop validation test cases from literature

## Ongoing Activities

### Continuous Collection
- Monitor new publications in relevant journals
- Track conference proceedings
- Review newly published textbooks
- Follow research groups and institutions

### Collection Maintenance
- Update outdated materials
- Remove superseded references
- Consolidate duplicate resources
- Archive historical but non-current materials

### Knowledge Sharing
- Create reading guides for specific topics
- Develop literature review summaries
- Share key findings and insights
- Document lessons learned from literature

## Success Metrics

### Coverage Completeness
- Core textbooks acquired for each focus area
- Key research papers identified and collected
- Reference materials available for common calculations
- Industry standards documented

### Usability
- Easy navigation and retrieval of materials
- Clear organization by topic
- Comprehensive annotations available
- Quick reference guides created

### Application
- Literature directly supports calculator development
- Theoretical foundation validates flight system design
- Design decisions traceable to literature sources
- Performance predictions validated against published data
