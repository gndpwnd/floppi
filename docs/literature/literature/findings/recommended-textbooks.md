## 1. AEROSPACE ENGINEERING

### 1.1 Aerodynamics - Fundamental

#### **Fundamentals of Aerodynamics (6th Edition)**
- **Author:** John D. Anderson Jr.
- **Publisher:** McGraw-Hill Education (2016)
- **Why Recommended:**
  - Gold standard at MIT, Stanford, and virtually all top aerospace programs
  - Author is Glenn L. Martin Distinguished Professor at University of Maryland
  - Conversational, self-teaching writing style
  - 1,000+ pages with extensive illustrations
- **Relevant Topics:**
  - Inviscid incompressible flow (low-speed drone flight)
  - Airfoil theory and thin airfoil theory
  - Finite-wing theory (fixed-wing drones)
  - Viscous flow and boundary layers
- **Best For:** Deep dive into aerodynamics fundamentals

---

#### **Introduction to Flight (8th Edition)**
- **Author:** John D. Anderson Jr.
- **Publisher:** McGraw-Hill Education
- **Why Recommended:**
  - Designed for first/second-year students
  - Easy-to-understand writing style
  - Builds physical intuition with historical context
  - "The best intro to Aero book on the planet"
- **Relevant Topics:**
  - Basic aerodynamics principles
  - Flight dynamics fundamentals
  - Propulsion basics
  - Aircraft performance
- **Best For:** Building foundational understanding before tackling advanced texts

---

#### **Low-Speed Aerodynamics (2nd Edition)**
- **Author:** Joseph Katz and Allen Plotkin
- **Publisher:** Cambridge University Press (Cambridge Aerospace Series #13)
- **Why Recommended:**
  - Specifically focused on low-speed flight (critical for drones)
  - Interweaves classical theory with modern computational methods
  - Used in senior and graduate-level courses
- **Relevant Topics:**
  - Inviscid, incompressible, irrotational aerodynamics
  - Wing theory for low-speed flight
  - Panel methods and computational techniques
  - Ground effect (relevant for landing)
- **Best For:** Modern drone design using CFD

---

#### **Aerodynamics for Engineering Students (8th Edition)**
- **Author:** Steven H. Collicott (Ph.D., Stanford), Daniel T. Valentine, E.L. Houghton, P.W. Carpenter
- **Publisher:** Elsevier
- **Why Recommended:**
  - Lead author has Ph.D. from Stanford
  - World's leading aerodynamics course text
  - MATLAB integration for computational work
- **Relevant Topics:**
  - Concise explanations of basic concepts
  - Compressible and incompressible flow
  - Worked examples in SI and Imperial units
- **Best For:** University-level aerodynamics course
