# Literature Collection Scope

## Overview
This document defines the scope of the literature and research collection for the floppi project. The collection serves as the theoretical foundation for both the flight calculator and flight control systems development.

## In Scope

### Aerospace Engineering
- Fundamental principles of flight
- Aircraft design and performance analysis
- Flight dynamics and control theory
- Stability and control systems
- Aerospace structures and materials

### Aerodynamics
- Fluid dynamics fundamentals
- Airfoil theory and design
- Computational fluid dynamics (CFD)
- Low Reynolds number aerodynamics
- Boundary layer theory
- Vortex dynamics

### Rotary-Wing/Rotor Aircraft
- Helicopter theory and design
- Rotor aerodynamics and dynamics
- Blade element momentum theory
- Rotor performance analysis
- Autorotation principles
- Multi-rotor configurations

### VTOL Systems
- Vertical takeoff and landing principles
- Transition flight mechanics
- Tiltrotor and tiltwing systems
- Hybrid VTOL configurations
- Control strategies for VTOL aircraft
- Hover and forward flight dynamics

### Mechanical Engineering
- Structural mechanics and analysis
- Materials science and selection
- Mechanical vibrations
- Machine design principles
- Stress and strain analysis
- Composite materials

### Electrical Engineering
- Power electronics
- Motor control systems
- Electrical machines (brushless motors)
- Control systems theory
- Signal processing
- Embedded systems design

### Battery Chemistry
- Lithium-ion battery technology
- Battery management systems (BMS)
- Electrochemistry fundamentals
- Battery performance characteristics
- Charging and discharging dynamics
- Thermal management
- Energy density and power density analysis

## Supporting Objectives

### Calculator Development
- Provide theoretical basis for flight performance calculations
- Support aerodynamic modeling and analysis
- Enable accurate battery and power system sizing
- Inform structural design calculations

### Flight Systems Development
- Ground control system design in flight dynamics theory
- Support sensor fusion and state estimation algorithms
- Enable advanced control law development
- Provide foundation for autonomous flight capabilities

## Out of Scope

### Excluded Topics
- General electronics tutorials (basic circuit theory, breadboarding guides)
  - Rationale: Core EE textbooks already cover fundamental electrical concepts
- Hobbyist drone build guides
  - Rationale: Focus is on theoretical foundation, not step-by-step assembly
- Generic programming tutorials
  - Rationale: Software development resources maintained separately
- Consumer drone operation manuals
  - Rationale: Not relevant to custom design and development
- Unrelated engineering disciplines (chemical, civil, etc.)

## Collection Priorities

### Primary Focus
1. Textbooks and academic literature with rigorous theoretical treatment
2. Research papers addressing specific design challenges
3. Technical reference materials with validated data and equations
4. Industry standards and best practices documentation

### Secondary Focus
1. Historical perspectives on aircraft development
2. Case studies of successful VTOL implementations
3. Experimental results and flight test data
4. Design methodologies and optimization techniques

## Quality Standards

### Source Credibility
- Academic textbooks from recognized publishers
- Peer-reviewed journal articles
- Conference proceedings from established organizations
- Technical reports from aerospace institutions
- Industry-standard reference materials

### Content Requirements
- Mathematical rigor and derivations
- Clear notation and terminology
- Validated experimental or computational results
- Comprehensive references and citations
- Practical applicability to design problems

## Maintenance and Updates
- Regular review of collection relevance
- Addition of newly published significant works
- Removal of outdated or superseded materials
- Annotation of particularly useful sections or chapters
- Cross-referencing related topics across sources
