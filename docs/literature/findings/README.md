# Literature Research Findings

This directory contains comprehensive research on recommended textbooks and literature for drone development.

## Documents

### [recommended-textbooks.md](recommended-textbooks.md)
Comprehensive compilation of all textbook recommendations across all disciplines. This is the main reference document.

**Organized by:**
- Aerospace Engineering (Aerodynamics, Flight Dynamics, Aircraft Design, UAV-specific)
- Mechanical Engineering (Mechanics of Materials, Machine Design, Structural Analysis, Vibrations, Materials Science)
- Electrical Engineering (Power Electronics, Motor Drives, Embedded Systems, Sensors, Control Systems)
- Battery & Energy Storage (Electrochemistry, Li-ion/LiPo, BMS, Power Management)
- Rotary-Wing Aircraft (Helicopter Theory, Multi-rotor Aerodynamics)

### Individual Discipline Reports
- `aerospace-textbooks.md` - Detailed aerospace engineering resources
- `mechanical-textbooks.md` - Mechanical engineering for drone structures
- `electrical-textbooks.md` - Electrical systems and electronics
- `battery-textbooks.md` - Battery technology and energy storage
- `rotary-wing-textbooks.md` - Rotorcraft and helicopter theory

## Research Methodology

All recommendations based on:
- Adoption at top universities (MIT, Stanford, Caltech, Cornell, ERAU, Georgia Tech, etc.)
- Industry reviews and professional society recommendations (AIAA, IEEE)
- Academic citations and research usage
- Author credentials from leading institutions
- Verified purchaser reviews from professionals
