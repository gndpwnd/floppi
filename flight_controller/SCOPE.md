# Embedded VTOL Flight Controller - Quaternion attitude, PID/LQR/MPC, IMU sensor fusion, acrobatic trajectory planning — Scope

## Objective <!-- repo-schema:objective -->

Embedded VTOL flight controller firmware covering attitude estimation, control algorithms, sensor fusion, and trajectory planning


## Research Topics <!-- repo-schema:research-topics -->

- **Attitude Estimation**
  - Quaternion-based attitude representation
  - Complementary and Mahony filters
  - Extended Kalman Filter for orientation
  - Gyroscope bias estimation
- **Flight Control Algorithms**
  - PID tuning for multirotor control
  - LQR state-space control design
  - Model Predictive Control for UAVs
  - Rate and angle mode controllers
- **IMU Sensor Fusion**
  - Accelerometer and gyroscope fusion
  - Magnetometer integration and calibration
  - Barometer altitude estimation
  - Multi-sensor redundancy
- **Acrobatic Trajectory Planning**
  - Aggressive maneuver planning
  - Minimum-snap trajectory generation
  - Flip and roll trajectory optimization
  - Safety envelope and recovery
