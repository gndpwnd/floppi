# DARPA Lift Challenge - Requirements & Competition Tracker

## Challenge Overview
**Competition Date:** Summer 2026  
**Prize Pool:** $6.5 million  
**Goal:** Achieve maximum payload-to-weight ratio in vertical lift aviation

---

## Competition Requirements

### Core Specifications
- **Maximum Drone Weight:** 55 lbs (excluding payload)
- **Minimum Payload:** 110 lbs (Olympic barbell plates)
- **Minimum Qualifying Ratio:** 2:1 (110 lbs payload / 55 lbs drone)
- **Target Competitive Ratio:** 4:1+ (220+ lbs payload)
- **Takeoff/Landing:** Vertical, unassisted
- **Aircraft Type:** Heavier-than-air drone

### Course Requirements
- **Distance:** 5 nautical miles (9.26 km)
- **Altitude:** 350 feet AGL
- **Payload Segment:** 4 nautical miles (carry payload)
- **Unloaded Segment:** 1 nautical mile (after payload drop)
- **Time Limit:** Under 30 minutes for qualifying run

### Payload Specifications
- Commercially available Olympic barbell gym plates
- Standard dimensions for consistency
- Can practice with same equipment before competition

### Regulatory Requirements
- FAA Part 107 Certification
- Experimental Airworthiness Certificate
- Commercial Drone License
