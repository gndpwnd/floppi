# 3D Multi-Rotor Frame Design - Aerodynamic design, vibration isolation, 3D-printable structural analysis, sensor placement — Scope

## Objective <!-- repo-schema:objective -->

3D frame design for multi-rotor drones covering aerodynamics, vibration isolation, structural analysis, and sensor placement


## Research Topics <!-- repo-schema:research-topics -->

- **Aerodynamic Frame Design**
  - Drag reduction for multirotor frames
  - Propeller-frame interaction effects
  - Ducted vs open propeller configurations
  - Airflow optimization around frame
- **Vibration Isolation**
  - Motor vibration damping methods
  - Soft-mount and hard-mount strategies
  - Resonance frequency analysis
  - Vibration impact on IMU sensors
- **3D-Printable Structural Analysis**
  - FDM material selection (PLA, PETG, Nylon, CF)
  - Layer orientation and strength optimization
  - FEA for 3D-printed drone frames
  - Weight-to-strength ratio optimization
- **Sensor Placement**
  - Optimal IMU mounting location
  - GPS antenna placement considerations
  - Camera and lidar integration mounts
  - EMI shielding for sensitive electronics
